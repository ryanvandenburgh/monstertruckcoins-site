How to play

  • Right arrow is gas, left arrow is brake and reverse, and the up arrow hops.
  • Hop over rocks: each hit costs a heart.
  • Every pit needs a hop to clear it.
  • Keep holding gas in the air. Letting go slows you down.
  • Land on your wheels. Flipping too far over is a crash.

Common questions

How do I unlock trucks and levels?
Every coin you collect in Adventure mode goes into your coin bank, even if you crash. Pick a locked truck or level on the main menu and press UNLOCK. Levels unlock in order.

Why can't I pick Normal or Hard?
Each level starts on Easy. Beat Easy on a level to unlock Normal there, then beat Normal to unlock Hard.

Do Endless coins count?
No. Endless is about distance. Only Adventure coins go into the bank.

There's no sound.
Check Settings (the gear icon) and make sure Sound is ON.

Will I lose my progress?
Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
