Privacy Policy

Monster Truck CoinsSupport

Effective October 1, 2026. This policy covers the Monster Truck Coins app for iPhone and iPad.

Short version: Monster Truck Coins does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No internet connection is used during play. The app does not send data anywhere.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

What stays on your device

To work as a game, the app saves a few things only on your device: your sound setting, your coins and unlocked trucks, levels and difficulties, your best Endless distance, and a play history (levels played and how each run ended) shown on the in-game Stats screen. None of this leaves your device unless you choose to copy the Stats text and share it yourself. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds features that handle data), this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
